# Abide in His Word — daily live Rosary

From 1 November 2026, every day at 6 PM Singapore time, this repository streams a pray-along Holy Rosary to the
[Abide in His Word](https://www.youtube.com/@abideinhiswords) YouTube channel.

Mysteries by day: Monday and Saturday — Joyful; Tuesday and Friday — Sorrowful; Wednesday and Sunday — Glorious;
Thursday — Luminous.

The videos combine our own voice recording with public-domain sacred paintings (credits in each YouTube description).
